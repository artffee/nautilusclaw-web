Before you begin.

The guest adventure and the signed-in chat beta handle information differently. Here is what this version does.

Privacy & your information

No ads. No data resale. We do not display advertising or sell your family’s personal data. Accounts and billing are managed by a parent or guardian. The service providers and cloud processing used for signed-in features are described below; guest learning does not require a name, account, or microphone.

Free math, alphabet, word & pattern games

The Play & learn area uses written content and puzzles generated in the browser. Your age-level choice, answers, and round progress stay in page memory and reset when you leave or reload. These games do not load account sign-in, analytics, or payment code and do not save a learning record. Hosting still involves ordinary web requests. Optional read-aloud uses your browser voice; processing depends on the browser and selected voice.

Storybook adventures

The Storybook Club offers written planet, space, forest, dinosaur, and castle stories with fixed choices, age-adjusted text, and a number or planet-order puzzle. Nia and the Planet Parade uses real planet names and their order from the Sun; its drawings are not to scale. These are make-believe stories, not science simulations. Story choices and progress stay in page memory and reset when you leave or reload. No account, analytics, AI service, or saved reading record is used. The final screen can print the complete chosen story and an offline activity. Optional read-aloud uses your browser voice.

The Curious Reef guest adventure

The Curious Reef asks for an age band and fixed story choices, not a name, email, birthday, microphone recording, or free-text message. Answers stay in page memory while you play. On completion, a personal recap is kept in sessionStorage for this browser tab, including across page navigation and reloads. It is cleared when the tab’s session ends, unless your browser restores the session.

A grown-up may choose to save a recap in this browser. It contains the age band, completion date, camouflage answer attempts, counting answer attempts and hints used, topics, and story choices. Only these fixed-choice learning results are kept; no personal identifiers or tracking are added. It is not linked to a parent account and is not sent to our account database or an AI provider. Other people using the same browser can see it. Remove it from the personal recap view or clear this site's browser storage.

The adventure, personal recap, and sample recap do not load account sign-in code or analytics. The sample at /parent-preview always shows fictional examples, never your saved recap. Hosting still involves ordinary web requests, which can include technical information such as an IP address and browser information. Optional read-aloud uses the browser's speech service; processing depends on the browser and selected voice.

Parent accounts and the chat beta

Parent sign-in uses Supabase. Your parent email and authentication information, parent name, child nickname and age band, saved chat history, progress, and automated flags may be stored through that service. Signed-in progress is cloud-backed; it is not stored only on your device.

AI chat sends conversation text through this site's server to the configured AI provider, currently Anthropic. Safety processing may also run on the server. Browser voice input may be processed by your browser vendor's speech service. Use a nickname, avoid sensitive personal information, and do not share addresses, school details, contact details, passwords, or private photographs.

We do not promise that AI conversation text remains on the device. The child chat beta is intended for use with a parent or guardian; children should not create their own accounts.

Parent billing

If you subscribe, Stripe processes payment details and stores billing records such as your parent email, customer identifier, subscription, and invoices. Our server links your parent account identifier to a Stripe customer to verify access. Child names, profiles, and conversation content are not sent to Stripe by this billing integration. Card details are entered on Stripe-hosted pages and are not stored by this app. See Stripe’s privacy policy. Clearing learning data does not cancel a subscription or remove payment records. Manage the subscription separately from the billing page.

Access and clearing saved information

The account dashboard can export saved learning data and clear the child profile, chat history, progress, and flags associated with that account. Clearing this learning data retains the parent sign-in account. It does not erase separately saved guest recaps or provider operational logs. Full authentication-account deletion is not available through this interface. No claim is made that a browser action deletes every provider backup or log.

Recaps saved by a grown-up in localStorage are kept until removed or the browser clears site storage. Unsaved recaps last only for the browser tab’s session. Signed-in learning data is retained until cleared through the account dashboard; provider logs and backups follow the relevant provider's retention practices. See Supabase's privacy policy, Anthropic's privacy policy, and Vercel's privacy policy for those services.

Safety & adult involvement

The guided adventure uses written content and fixed choices. The separate chat beta may generate inaccurate, unsuitable, or unexpected replies. A parent or trusted grown-up should supervise and discuss the experience, especially with younger children.

Automated flags are imperfect and may miss important context. They are not an emotion diagnosis, developmental assessment, emergency service, or reliable monitoring system. There is no promise of an immediate alert to a parent.

If a child is upset or shares something concerning, pause the activity and help them talk with a trusted adult. For immediate danger, contact local emergency services. This product does not replace professional care.

Take breaks. Each guest expedition has a clear ending and an offline activity. No daily streak is required to finish it.

What works today

  • Free learning games: counting, addition, subtraction, multiplication and division; all 26 alphabet letters; matching, beginning-letter and spelling rounds; shape and number patterns. Three age-guide levels, hints, and short sessions with off-screen activities.
  • Storybook adventures: five original adventures—planets, space, forest, dinosaurs, and a magical castle—with three reading levels, two story choices, a number or planet-order puzzle, different endings, optional read-aloud, and a printable full story. Progress resets when you leave.
  • Guest ocean adventure: three age levels, science discovery, number puzzle, branching story choices, read-aloud where supported, and an offline activity.
  • Guest recap: actual session answers and hints, optional device-only saving, a separate sample dashboard, removal, and personal recap printing.
  • Parent accounts: existing Supabase sign-in and password recovery, saved learning activity, export, and clearing learning data.
  • Chat beta: existing server-based AI connection. Service availability must be confirmed in the app. Written sample replies are identified when AI is unavailable.

We are not advertising native App Store or Google Play downloads, a school console, an offline AI USB bundle, clinical monitoring, or instant parent alerts as available features.
