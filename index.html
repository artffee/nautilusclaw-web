/01 — How it works

Speak.
Listen.
Grow.

No typing. No tapping through menus. A child holds a microphone, asks a question, and the conversation begins. Behind the friendly voice: age-calibrated prompts, on-the-fly emotion analysis, and a parent who can read every word.

01

One tap, one voice.

The Web Speech API turns speech into text in real time. No keyboards, no logins for the child — just hold the mic and talk.

02

Calibrated for the age in the room.

Every reply is shaped by an age-aware system prompt — three sentences for a five-year-old, four for a nine-year-old. Never markdown, never lists, never adult tone.

03

Quietly aware of feelings.

Each utterance runs a parallel emotion analysis. Three negative emotions in a row? A note appears in the parent dashboard. Crisis phrases? Immediate intervention.

/02 — Age-adaptive intelligence

Three voices,
one Nautilus.

A three-year-old asking about clouds and a twelve-year-old debugging fractions need entirely different teachers. NautilusClaw is three teachers, switched by a single setting.

/ Tier 01

Little Explorer

Pre-literate. Voice only. One- or two-sentence answers. Nautilus speaks in tiny, cheerful words a child can chew on.

/ Tier 02

Explorer

Ages 6–8

Early readers. Two- to three-sentence replies. Fun, enthusiastic, elementary-level vocabulary.

Sam, 7
How does an octopus change colour?
Nautilus
Octopuses have tiny color bags in their skin called chromatophores! They squeeze and stretch them to match what's around. Like a living costume change!
/ Tier 03

Champion

Ages 9–12

Independent learners. Up to four sentences. Middle-school vocabulary, real reasoning, no markdown — just talk.

Theo, 11
Why don't whales drown when they sleep?
Nautilus
Whales sleep with one half of their brain at a time — it's called unihemispheric sleep. The awake half keeps them swimming up for breaths every few minutes. Imagine taking a nap while still walking home!

/05 — Built for childhood, regulated for it

Private by
design.

We chose a harder architecture so your child gets a softer experience. Data lives on your device by default. The cloud is opt-in. The audit trail is yours.

P · 01
Local-first storage

All progress, mood entries, and chat history stored on the device. Cloud sync is opt-in, never default.

P · 02
Offline-capable

Run entirely from a USB drive with a local Ollama LLM. No internet, no API key, no exfiltration risk.

P · 03
Crisis detection

15 trigger phrases scanned on every utterance — bypassing the LLM. Parents are notified instantly with context.

P · 04
Right to be forgotten

One-click GDPR Article 17 erasure. Cascade deletion across every table, every device.
