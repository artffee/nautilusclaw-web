Before we dive in.

Can we try it without creating an account?

Yes. The guided adventure asks only for an age band. Your choices stay in the current page unless a grown-up chooses to save the recap on this device.

Is the guest adventure powered by AI?

No. It uses written educational content and a story assembled from your choices. The separate signed-in chat app can use AI when the server service is available.

Where does our information go?

Guest adventure choices are not sent to an AI service or account database. Signed-in profiles and chat history are stored through Supabase, and AI chat text is processed through the server and AI provider. Read the privacy information.

Does it work on a phone?

The adventure works in a browser on a phone, tablet, or computer. No microphone is required. Optional read-aloud uses your browser's speech feature when available.

Does Nautilus monitor my child's wellbeing?

No. Automated chat flags can miss things or misread context. They are not a diagnosis, monitoring service, or substitute for a trusted adult. Read the safety notes.
