What does the yearly price mean?

The yearly plan is one payment of $59 every year, not $4.92 charged monthly. Twelve monthly payments would cost $83.88, so yearly billing saves $24.88 (about 29%). Both options include the same features.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Open Manage billing in the parent area to cancel through Stripe. Cancellation stops the next renewal; paid access continues until the end of the current billing period. Canceling does not automatically refund earlier payments.

Is Family available today?

The availability notice above reflects checkout readiness. Until checkout opens, no Family payments can be taken here. The free guest adventure remains available.

Is the AI chat suitable for unsupervised use?

The chat is a learning beta for use together with a parent or guardian. AI can make mistakes and service availability may vary. It is not a wellbeing monitoring service. Read the safety notes.

What happens to our learning history if we cancel?

You can still sign in to view, export, or clear your saved learning activity. The free guest adventure stays available. AI access ends when your paid period ends.
